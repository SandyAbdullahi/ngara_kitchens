FAQs

Have some questions? We have answers.

Do you cater outside Nairobi?

We focus on Nairobi and nearby counties, but can travel further for larger events. Share your venue and we’ll confirm logistics.

What group sizes can you handle?

Anything from chef-at-home dinners for 8 to multi-course galas for 500. We scale the team, service style, and kitchen kit to fit.

Can you accommodate dietary needs?

Yes. We regularly design menus around gluten-free, vegan, halal, and allergy-friendly requirements—just share the details 48 hours ahead.

Do you provide staff, bar, or rentals?

We can staff the entire service (chefs, servers, bartenders) and arrange glassware, crockery, cutlery, and bar packages to match your event.

How soon can you respond?

We aim to reply within 24 hours with a tailored proposal. For urgent events, call us and we’ll prioritise a same-day response.
