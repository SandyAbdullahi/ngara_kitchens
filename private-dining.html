How we host

We bring a full kitchen setup—burners, mise en place, serviceware—and cook every course in your home. You choose the level of interaction: chef’s counter storytelling or quiet, unobtrusive execution.

  • Seasonal, multi-course tasting menus with optional wine pairing.
  • Table styling with linens and florals on request.
  • Professional servers and bartenders to keep the night effortless.
  • Dietary notes handled in advance so every guest feels cared for.

Sample flow

A night with KOMÈ

  • Arrival with welcome bite and a signature spritz.
  • Seasonal starter: e.g., charred aubergine with tahini and pomegranate.
  • Main feature: slow-roasted lamb shoulder with herb salsa, or miso-glazed salmon.
  • Sides that shine: vibrant salads and roasted market vegetables.
  • Sweet finish: flourless chocolate cake or citrus tart with whipped mascarpone.

Good to know

  • We can serve 8–40 guests comfortably in-home.
  • Staffing is tailored: chef-only, chef plus server, or full team.
  • We handle rentals (glassware, plates, flatware) if you need them.
  • We clear down and leave the kitchen as we found it.
